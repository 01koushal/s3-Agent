Cardiovascular diseases are disorders of the heart and blood vessels. Important risk factors can include
high blood pressure, abnormal blood cholesterol, tobacco use, diabetes, physical inactivity, unhealthy diet,
and family history. Atherosclerosis is the buildup of plaque in artery walls and can restrict blood flow.
Warning signs of a heart attack may include chest discomfort, shortness of breath, and discomfort in the
arms, back, neck, jaw, or stomach. Stroke warning signs can include sudden face drooping, arm weakness,
speech difficulty, sudden vision changes, or loss of balance. Suspected heart attack or stroke requires
urgent emergency medical attention. This text is educational and does not provide diagnosis or treatment.
