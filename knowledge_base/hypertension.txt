Hypertension means persistently elevated blood pressure. It often has no obvious symptoms, which is why
measurement is important. Risk factors can include age, family history, high sodium intake, low physical
activity, excess body weight, tobacco use, and some chronic conditions.
Uncontrolled hypertension can increase the risk of cardiovascular disease, stroke, and kidney disease.
Blood pressure management may involve lifestyle changes and, when prescribed, medication. Lifestyle
approaches can include reducing dietary sodium, regular physical activity, limiting alcohol, avoiding tobacco,
and following a balanced eating pattern. A healthcare professional should interpret blood pressure readings
and recommend an appropriate care plan. This document is general educational information only.
